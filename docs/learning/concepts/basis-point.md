# Basis point (bp, bps)

1 bp = 0.01% = 0.0001. Fees, spreads, and slippage are quoted in bps because they are tiny
fractions of notional. A 60 bp fee on a $1,000 trade is $6.

Why it matters: costs and edge live on the same scale. If a signal predicts a 2 bp move and
each side costs 40 bp in fees, the signal is worthless however accurate it is.
