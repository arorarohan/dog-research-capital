# Arbitrage

Profit from price inconsistencies with (nearly) no risk. Example on prediction markets: if
mutually exclusive, exhaustive outcomes have asks summing to 0.96, buying all of them costs
0.96 and pays exactly 1.00. Real-world arbitrage is eaten by fees, resolution ambiguity,
thin depth, and execution risk (one leg fills, the other doesn't), so "arb" usually means
"edge net of those frictions".
